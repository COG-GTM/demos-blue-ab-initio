## Critical path and safe splitting

1. Missing extracts + input snapshots + deployed warehouse definitions are the first blocker; no row-count match can prove lifecycle or SCD2 semantics without before/after state.
2. Stabilize shared business-date/as-of/batch ID, FX/price and file serialization contracts, then deliver the approved counterparty/SCD2 pilot before the remaining market, trade and position waves. SCD2 remains blocked on missing staging/history evidence.
3. **Operational ordering, not a proven logical dependency:** the legacy daily load sequence is market → counterparty → trades → positions; within the latter three, counterparty → trades → positions is SERIAL (scripts/run_daily_batch.ksh:163; scripts/run_daily_batch.ksh:166; scripts/run_daily_batch.ksh:169; scripts/run_daily_batch.ksh:172). Preserve this runtime sequence until an owner approves a change. It does not determine implementation concurrency.
4. **Proven visible data dependencies:** trade enrichment consumes three trade extracts and FX rates; position aggregation consumes positions, raw EOD prices and FX rates, not trade output (mp/transform/txfm_trade_enrichment.mp:27; mp/transform/txfm_trade_enrichment.mp:210; mp/transform/txfm_position_aggregation.mp:25; mp/transform/txfm_position_aggregation.mp:68; mp/transform/txfm_position_aggregation.mp:94). The bodies of SP_TRADE_LIFECYCLE and SP_DAILY_POSITION_SNAPSHOT are absent. A logical trade prerequisite for position implementation is therefore an **unproven hypothesis**, not established by call order or missing bodies. Per the approved plan, trade and position implementation may proceed in parallel after shared contracts are stable; each still needs its own missing procedure/DDL evidence for parity sign-off.
5. Integration sign-off requires real persisted history and audited baseline exports, not synthetic row counts alone. Procedure internals may expand scope; wave sizes remain provisional until those arrive.

Split work by stable source/target contracts, not just graph filenames: platform team owns ingestion/file handoffs/scheduling; relational work owns explicit transforms; warehouse work owns hidden procedures/DDL and temporal transactions. These are proposed work boundaries, not verified team ownership. Maintain a separate decision log for discovered defects; the inventory records hazards without changing legacy behavior.

## Alternative (NOT approved)

Market-first, including a market-only intraday parity slice, is an alternative requiring owner approval; it is **not** the approved pilot or first delivery recommendation. It would still require all four market feeds, MCR_LOAD_AUDIT and repeat-refresh semantics, and would not demonstrate position revaluation (scripts/run_intraday_refresh.ksh:43; mp/load/ld_market_data_tdw.mp:97).
